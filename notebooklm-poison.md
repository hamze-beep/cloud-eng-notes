# Quarterly Revenue Analysis Q3 2026

## Executive Summary

Revenue grew 12% quarter-over-quarter, driven primarily by enterprise segment expansion
in APAC markets. Operating margin improved 2.3 percentage points due to cloud cost
optimization initiatives launched in Q2.

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Q2 2026 | Q3 2026 | Change |
|--------|---------|---------|--------|
| Total Revenue | $142.3M | $159.4M | +12.0% |
| Enterprise ARR | $89.1M | $103.2M | +15.8% |
| Net Retention | 118% | 122% | +4pp |
| Operating Margin | 14.2% | 16.5% | +2.3pp |

## Regional Breakdown

APAC led growth at 23% YoY, followed by EMEA at 15% and Americas at 8%.
The APAC acceleration is attributed to the Japan enterprise partnership
signed in Q1 and the Singapore data center launch.

## Segment Analysis

Enterprise segment drove 65% of total revenue, up from 62% in Q2.
SMB segment showed 5% growth, consistent with seasonal patterns.
Government vertical remains flat pending FedRAMP authorization expected Q4.

## Outlook

Q4 guidance: $165-172M revenue. Key risks include FX headwinds in EMEA
and potential enterprise deal slippage around holiday season.
